# Paperless-ngx

Paperless turns paper into searchable files. Upload a scan or a photo of a letter, receipt, or bill, and Paperless reads
the text, files it, and lets you find it again by searching for any word in it.

**Address:** <https://paperless.@domain@>

## Signing in

Go to <https://paperless.@domain@>. You're sent to Authentik to sign in, then straight into Paperless — there's no
separate Paperless password.

!!! warning "First time? You may see an empty page"

    Your Paperless profile is created the first time you sign in, but it starts with no permissions. If the dashboard
    is blank or says you don't have permission, [let @admin@ know](../help.md) so they can switch it on.

!!! note "Use the website"

    Paperless phone apps expect a Paperless username and password, which your account doesn't have, so they won't
    connect here. The website works well on a phone browser — add it to your home screen for quick access.

## Adding documents

- **Drag and drop** files (PDFs, photos, Word documents) anywhere onto the **Dashboard**, or click **Upload** on the
  Documents page.
- **On a phone:** open the website, tap **Upload**, and choose **Take Photo** to snap a document straight from the
  camera.

It takes a minute or so for Paperless to read a new document. It then appears under **Documents**.

## Finding documents

- Type anything into the **search bar** at the top — Paperless searches the full text of every document, not just the
  title.
- Narrow things down with **tags**, **correspondents** (who it's from), and **document types** (bill, receipt, letter…).
  Paperless learns to fill these in automatically as you use them.

## Who can see my documents?

Documents you upload are yours. To share one with someone else here, open it and use **Permissions** to give them
access.
