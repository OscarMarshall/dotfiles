# Storyteller

Storyteller combines an **ebook** with its **audiobook** so you can read along while you listen — the words highlight as
they're read aloud, and you can switch between reading and listening without losing your place.

**Address:** <https://storyteller.@domain@>

## In a browser

1. Go to <https://storyteller.@domain@>.
2. Sign in with **Authentik**.
3. Pick a book from your library and open it to read and listen right in the browser.

## On your phone

1. Install **Storyteller** from the iOS App Store or Google Play.
2. For the server, enter `https://storyteller.@domain@`.
3. Sign in with **Authentik**.
4. Tap a book to **download** it, then open it to read and listen — it works offline once downloaded.

## Where the books come from

Books are added by @admin@. If there's one you'd like, [ask](../help.md) — Storyteller needs both the ebook and the
audiobook of the same edition, so not every book can be added.

!!! tip "Other e-readers"

    Storyteller's synced books are ordinary EPUB files with "read-aloud" built in. Download one from the web page and
    any e-reader app that supports EPUB 3 read-aloud (such as Apple Books) can play it too.
