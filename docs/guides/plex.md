# Plex

Plex is a second way to watch the movies and shows here. Unlike everything else on this site, it doesn't use your
[account](../account.md) — it uses your own free **Plex account**, and @admin@ shares the library with you.

**Most people should use [Jellyfin](jellyfin.md) instead.** Plex is here for devices where Plex's app works better, or
if you're already used to it.

## Getting access

1. Make a free account at [plex.tv](https://www.plex.tv/) if you don't have one.
2. Send @admin@ the **email address or username** of your Plex account and ask to be added.
3. You'll get an email from Plex inviting you to the server. Click **Accept**.

## Watching

- **In a browser:** go to [app.plex.tv](https://app.plex.tv/) and sign in with your Plex account.
- **On a phone, TV, or streaming stick:** install the **Plex** app and sign in with the same Plex account.

Either way, the shared libraries show up in the sidebar under the server's name.

!!! tip "Hide the ads and free movies"

    Plex mixes in its own free movies and live TV. To see just this server's library, open **More** (or the
    sidebar) and **pin** the shared libraries, then unpin Plex's own sections.

## Requests

To ask for a movie or show that isn't in the library, use [Seerr](seerr.md). New things appear in Plex too once they're
ready.
