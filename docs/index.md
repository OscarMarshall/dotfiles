# Welcome

This site explains how to use the services @admin@ runs for friends and family: watching movies and shows, backing up
your photos, storing files, reading and listening to books, and playing on the game servers.

## Start here

1. **[Get your account](account.md).** One account signs you in to almost everything. You'll get it from an invite link
   or by signing in with Discord.
2. **[See what's available](services.md).** Every service you can use, with its address and a link to its guide.
3. **Pick a guide** from the menu for step-by-step setup, including the phone and TV apps.

## The most popular things

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **Watch something** — [Jellyfin](guides/jellyfin.md) streams movies and shows to your browser, phone, or TV.
- **Ask for something new** — [Seerr](guides/seerr.md) lets you request a movie or show; it appears in Jellyfin once
  it's ready.
- **Back up your photos** — [Immich](guides/immich.md) uploads your phone's camera roll automatically.
- **Store and share files** — [Nextcloud](guides/nextcloud.md) is a private Dropbox with Word/Excel-style editing.

</div>
